FIELD STUDIO · PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Field Studio is an AI creative workspace operated as an independent software project. This policy explains how Field Studio handles information when you connect a Pinterest account through the official Pinterest OAuth authorization flow.

Information we receive

When you authorize Pinterest, Field Studio may receive the Pinterest account identifier, public account name, authorized scopes, and the Pins or Boards needed for a feature you request. We may also receive the image URL, title, source URL, and basic metadata for references you choose to import.

What we do not collect

Field Studio does not ask for or collect your Pinterest password, browser cookies, session cookies, or private browser history. We do not sell Pinterest data or use it for advertising.

How we use information

We use authorized information only to provide the connected Field Studio features: showing selected Pins or Boards, importing requested visual references, maintaining your connection, and preventing duplicate imports. We do not publish Pins, edit Boards, or take other actions on your behalf unless a future feature clearly requests separate permission and you explicitly approve it.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are handled by the Field Studio server and are not displayed in the browser interface. Imported reference metadata and images are retained in the workspace so you can use them later. You can disconnect Pinterest from Field Studio at any time; you can also request removal of connected-account data and imported references through the contact link below.

Third-party services

Field Studio uses Pinterest APIs to provide the connection you authorize. Pinterest handles its own account, authentication, and privacy practices under its own policies. This information page is hosted through GitHub Pages as a static public website.

Contact and deletion requests

For privacy questions or a deletion request, open an issue in the public project repository: Field Studio privacy contact. Please do not post passwords, OAuth secrets, or access tokens in an issue.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when Field Studio changes its connected services or data practices. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
